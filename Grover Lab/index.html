Grover Lab: find one in N

An exact state-vector simulation. Click any bar to hide the marked item there, then step through the oracle and the diffusion to watch its amplitude climb.

How to read this page

The idea

There are N hidden slots and one is the target. Each bar is one slot, and its height is its amplitude. The chance of finding a slot is its amplitude squared. Grover's job is to make the target bar tall.

Try this

1. Click a bar to choose the target. 2. Press Step and watch the red bar flip down, then jump up. 3. Press Step twice more. 4. Press 1000 shots in the measurement panel.

What to watch

The dashed teal line is the mean. Diffusion mirrors every bar across it. The rotation plane shows the same thing as an arrow turning toward |t⟩, and the curve shows the odds rising, then falling if you go past the optimum.

The three steps

Hadamard gives every slot equal amplitude. Oracle flips the sign of the target. Diffusion reflects all amplitudes about the mean. Repeat oracle and diffusion about (π/4)√N times.

1 · Superposition

Hadamard gates put the register in an equal mix of all N = 2ⁿ states, each with amplitude 1/√N. The target is hidden among them and nothing yet favors it.

2 · Oracle

The oracle U_f flips the sign of the marked state's amplitude and leaves the rest alone. Probabilities do not change yet, but the mean amplitude drops slightly.

3 · Diffusion

Reflecting every amplitude about the mean, 2|s⟩⟨s| − I, turns the small dip of the target into a large gain. After about (π/4)√N rounds the target holds nearly all the probability. Keep going and it rotates past.
